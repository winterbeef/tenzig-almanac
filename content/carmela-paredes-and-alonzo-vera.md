A dossier, not a fragment — these two are alive and active in the present era, and this entry has no pre-Scream half. It's included because half the cluster has an opinion about the [[lycos|Great Archive]]'s internal politics, and these two names are why.

## Persons of Interest

```
PERSONS OF INTEREST DOSSIER

```

**Carmela Paredes** holds the rank of Exult Advocas among the Conservators — one of the Archive's senior missionary offices — and is currently preparing an Advocas Mission to Tabet, carrying a substantial store of Preceptor Knowledge to share openly with scholars there. She preaches what people are calling, not always kindly, "the new philosophy": that the Archive's knowledge belongs to everyone who wants it, not just the credentialed.

**Alonzo Vera** holds the countervailing rank of Libres Divant, and believes precisely the opposite — that knowledge freely given is knowledge that ends up in the wrong hands, and that the Archive's survival depends on restricting access to "the worthy." He has the backing of several older Conservators, and, by more than one account, sympathizers well outside the Archive itself.

> Met Paredes myself when the Guild was asked to consider escorting her mission. I don't know if I trust the plan. I trust her.
