## From the Field Notes of Surveyor-Adept Yuen Petrakis, Terran Mandate Cartographic Office

Ainduia Lari hosts one of the Mandate's smaller and, I am told, less prestigious postings — a containment and inspection garrison whose formal charter reads, in full, as the detection and neutralization of "proscribed technological hazard," a phrase broad enough to mean nearly anything and specific enough that nobody stationed here seems to enjoy discussing it over dinner.

The garrison commander was cordial but tight-lipped regarding recent operational tempo, which I am reliably informed has increased substantially this past year. When asked what sort of hazard warranted the increase, I was told only that "we hope you never need to know the answer to that," delivered with a smile that did not reach the eyes.

> **Corvid:** whatever this garrison was watching for, it evidently did not stop the one hazard that mattered most. I have found no record — none at all — of Ainduia Lari's containment charter ever addressing anything resembling the Scream itself, which either means the garrison never saw it coming, or saw it coming and could do nothing about it, or — and I confess this is the possibility I like least — the Scream was never within its mandate to watch for in the first place. Something did grow from this garrison's charter, much later, in the present era. I will not say more about it here than that it still exists, still watches, and still does not enjoy discussing its business over dinner.
>
> **Two-Coin:** I've dealt with the present-day version of whoever this is. Corvid's being polite. They are not fun people to owe a favor to.

## From a Recruitment Notice, Posted at Several Regional Shipyards

WANTED: personnel of sound character and stronger nerve for garrison assignment, Ainduia Lari station. Duties include technical inspection, hazard containment, and field response to incidents of a classified nature. Prior service in hazardous materials handling, xenobiology, or applied physics strongly preferred. Applicants should understand that successful candidates may be asked, on short notice, to travel to sites of considerable danger with considerably less information than they would prefer.

Compensation exceeds standard Mandate garrison scale. So, historically, has attrition.

*[hand unknown]*

> **Corvid:** I confess a grim fondness for this notice's honesty. Whatever else can be said of the people who staffed that garrison, they were not lied to about the job.
>
> **Cinder:** "Compensation exceeds standard garrison scale. So, historically, has attrition." Read that twice. That's a government office admitting, in its own recruiting copy, that the pay is a bribe for a body count, and expecting applicants to read it as an honor instead. I've seen that exact bargain offered on [[cantho|Cantho]] and two other worlds, always dressed up in slightly nicer language, always meaning the same thing.
>
> **Two-Coin:** At least this one didn't bother dressing it up much. Compared to some of the recruiting notices I've read, that's practically an act of honesty.

## From a Private Letter, Recovered Among Garrison Effects

I cannot say much of what I do here, love, only that it matters more than most people will ever know, and that I sleep less well for knowing it. There are things loose in the wider dark that would make you very glad this garrison exists, and very unhappy to learn the details. I would rather you stayed unhappy in ignorance than unhappy in knowledge. Kiss the children for me.

*[hand unknown, signature illegible]*

> **Corvid:** I do not know who wrote this, or to whom, or whether either of them lived to see what came after. I include it because it is the only entry in this volume that made me set the page down for a moment before continuing.
>
> **Two-Coin:** Same. For once I've got nothing to add. Let it sit.
