## Field Notes

> **[FIELD NOTE — Surveyor-Adept Yuen Petrakis, Terran Mandate Cartographic Office, 2615.301]**
> Ainduia Lari hosts one of the Mandate's smaller and, I am told, less prestigious postings — a containment and inspection garrison whose formal charter reads, in full, as the detection and neutralization of "proscribed technological hazard," a phrase broad enough to mean nearly anything and specific enough that nobody stationed here seems to enjoy discussing it over dinner.
>
> The garrison commander was cordial but tight-lipped regarding recent operational tempo, which I am reliably informed has increased substantially this past year. When asked what sort of hazard warranted the increase, I was told only that "we hope you never need to know the answer to that," delivered with a smile that did not reach the eyes.

> **[FIELD NOTE — M. Okonkwo-Reyes, Archive Adept, 2nd Rank, 3176.091]**
> Whatever this garrison was watching for, it evidently did not stop the one hazard that mattered most. I have found no record of Ainduia Lari's containment charter ever addressing anything resembling the Scream itself — which either means the garrison never saw it coming, or saw it coming and could do nothing about it, or the Scream was simply never within its mandate to watch for. Something did grow from this garrison's charter, much later. I will say only that it still exists, still watches, and still does not enjoy discussing its business over dinner.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Cor. Dassin, Cerberus Corps field agent, 3188.170]**
> I've dealt with the present-day version of whoever this is. Okonkwo-Reyes is being polite about it. They are not fun people to owe a favor to. Filing that here for whoever reads this next.

## Recruitment Notice, Posted at Several Regional Shipyards

WANTED: personnel of sound character and stronger nerve for garrison assignment, Ainduia Lari station. Duties include technical inspection, hazard containment, and field response to incidents of a classified nature. Prior service in hazardous materials handling, xenobiology, or applied physics strongly preferred. Applicants should understand that successful candidates may be asked, on short notice, to travel to sites of considerable danger with considerably less information than they would prefer.

Compensation exceeds standard Mandate garrison scale. So, historically, has attrition.

*[hand unknown, undated]*

> **[FIELD NOTE — Tamsin Ilarra, salvage contractor, 3199.007]**
> Read that last line twice. That's a government office admitting, in its own recruiting copy, that the pay is a bribe for a body count, and expecting applicants to read it as an honor instead. I've seen that exact bargain offered on [[cantho|Cantho]] and elsewhere, always dressed up in slightly nicer language, always meaning the same thing.

## Private Letter, Recovered Among Garrison Effects

I cannot say much of what I do here, love, only that it matters more than most people will ever know, and that I sleep less well for knowing it. There are things loose in the wider dark that would make you very glad this garrison exists, and very unhappy to learn the details. I would rather you stayed unhappy in ignorance than unhappy in knowledge. Kiss the children for me.
