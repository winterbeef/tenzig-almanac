**Silverlight Amalgamated** is a research and applied-technology firm with holdings across the sector, publicly known for maltech-adjacent research programs it describes as strictly regulated and beneficial. **The New Dawn League** is an advocacy organization built around protecting psionic individuals from exploitation, and it treats Silverlight as its primary adversary — publicly, loudly, and with the kind of specificity that suggests they know things the rest of the sector doesn't.

Neither side's full story appears in this volume. What's here is what's said in the open.
