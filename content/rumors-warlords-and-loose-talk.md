title: "Rumors, Warlords, and Loose Talk"
---

A catch-all of dockside gossip, secondhand warnings, and stories too small or too strange to fit anywhere else in this volume. Most of what follows was never meant to be a formal record of anything — it is logged here in the same rough intelligence-log format the crews who first heard it would have used, reliability graded honestly where it can be.

## Overheard, Tavern Unnamed

```
UNVERIFIED INTELLIGENCE LOG
Reliability:   unverified, corroborated across three unrelated tellings
Location:      [[the-porth-belt|the belt out past Porth]]
Source:        tavern chatter, multiple sources
Logged:        3199.011
```

They say there's a corner of the belt out past Porth where ships go quiet and don't come back on schedule, not lost exactly, just late in a way nobody can explain after the fact. They say the crews that do come back don't talk about it, and the ones who talk too freely about it don't get hired again.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Tamsin Ilarra, salvage contractor, 3199.011]**
> I know a crew that came back late from that stretch once. Bought the pilot a drink and asked her about it, direct, no games. She looked at me for a long time and then asked if we could talk about literally anything else. We did. Never asked again.

## Warlord's Personal Correspondence, Recipient a Rival, Both Unnamed

```
UNVERIFIED INTELLIGENCE LOG
Reliability:   confirmed authentic, context unconfirmed
Location:      unrecorded
Source:        recovered correspondence
Logged:        undated
```

You call what I hold a "petty fiefdom" and I will grant you the pettiness is a matter of scale, not of ambition. I hold three worlds you do not, feed people you have let starve twice this decade, and sleep considerably better than a man who needs three separate food-tasters to trust his own table. Keep your insults. I will keep my worlds.

> **[FIELD NOTE — J. Praast, Mandate Cartographic Office (ret.), 3134.055]**
> Neither party to this correspondence appears by name in any other document gathered for this volume. Included purely because it is a wonderful piece of writing, and because it is a useful reminder that not every power in this cluster's history left a large enough mark to be remembered by anything but its own bragging.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Cor. Dassin, Cerberus Corps field agent, 3188.174]**
> Two men, each one certain his throne is the legitimate one because it happens to be smaller. I've got no more patience for a "petty fiefdom" lord than I do for a Concern boardroom. Same disease, different scale, same excuse for why the people under them go without.

## Children's Rhyme, Collected From Oral Tradition, World Unrecorded

```
UNVERIFIED INTELLIGENCE LOG
Reliability:   unverified, provenance unrecoverable
Location:      widely attested, no single origin
Source:        folklorist transcription
Logged:        3140.306
```

*Star to star and hull to hull,*
*mind the Song when the night is full,*
*close your eyes and count to nine,*
*the ones who hear it don't need eyes.*

> **[FIELD NOTE — Nine-Finger Sable, smuggler (self-described), 3140.306]**
> Taught this rhyme to a dockworker's kid once without thinking about where I'd learned it. Her mother was not pleased with me. Don't blame her, in hindsight. See [[the-song-beneath-the-stars|the entry on the Song]] for the more sober version of the same fear.

## Merchant's Marginal Note, Scrawled in the Corner of an Otherwise Unremarkable Cargo Manifest

```
UNVERIFIED INTELLIGENCE LOG
Reliability:   unverified, undated
Location:      unrecorded
Source:        marginal note, unattributed
Logged:        n/a
```

Strange feeling in the market today. Can't name it. Everyone a little too cheerful, like we all know something's coming and have collectively decided not to mention it. Sold everything at a fair price and left before dark anyway. Didn't used to be the sort of trader who left before dark.

> **[FIELD NOTE — R. Achterberg, independent hauler, 3142.081]**
> This note bears no date, no world, and no name, and I cannot prove it was written anywhere near the Scream at all. It's placed last in this volume regardless. Some fragments don't need a date to tell you exactly when they were written.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Whit, courier, 3122.026]**
> Whoever this was, they were right to leave before dark. I've learned to trust that exact feeling myself, more than once, and never once regretted listening to it.

> **[FIELD NOTE — M. Okonkwo-Reyes, Archive Adept, 2nd Rank, 3176.095]**
> Closing this volume out from my end, since it seems somebody should say something. Six hundred years on, I still don't know what any of it amounts to — but somebody was still writing "I have not yet received a reply" (see [[the-song-beneath-the-stars]]) right up until the day it stopped mattering, and somebody has to keep writing these things down so the next reader gets some kind of warning. That's the only faith I've got left in this project. It'll have to do.
