The Perimeter Agency contracts security, investigation, and enforcement services across the Tenzig sector — everything from convoy escort to bounty work to standing security details for corporate clients who can afford the rate. Its field arm, Cerberus Corps, has a reputation for competence and for a certain institutional coldness: agents are trained to treat every job as a contract with clear terms, not a cause. Whether that's reassuring or unsettling tends to depend on which side of the contract you're on.
