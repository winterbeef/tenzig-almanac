[[tabet|Tabet]] is loud, crowded, and full of people worth knowing before you meet them. This dossier covers one — a man impossible to avoid hearing about — and the small, exhausted circle that keeps him functional.

**Appius Faust** is a performance artist and provocateur working the Old Quarter, currently developing a new piece on social responsibility, individual choice, and fame — subject matter he discusses at a volume disproportionate to the size of any room he's in. Convinced of his own genius to a degree that reads, depending on the day, as either magnetic or exhausting. Rarely without a cigarette or a considerably worse habit. His fixation on the light of [[perope|Perope]] — and on whatever's been sitting in it for six hundred years — has become a recurring subject of his work.

His retinue:

**Vessia** — manager and handler, eleven years into a job that would have broken most people in one. Extraordinarily competent, visibly exhausted, and the only reason Faust hasn't ended up in front of a magistrate. Sizes up new arrivals fast, as either problems or solutions.

**Brin** — the newest addition, young, still a true believer, still in the phase where the genius excuses everything. Watch for the day that stops being true.

**"The One Who Knows"** — the longest-serving member of the retinue, unnamed by choice or by everyone else's habit. Says little. Watches Faust with an expression that stopped being admiration some time ago and hasn't yet become anything actionable.
