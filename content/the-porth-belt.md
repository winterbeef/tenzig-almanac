## Overview

*Mandate-era summary.* Working mineral belt, primary export cherambilth (unclassified resonant mineral, see Field Notes). Okotoks holds registered mining collective status under standard charter, self-governing, records available to claim-holders on request per charter terms. No anomalies flagged on initial pass; outer debris field not fully surveyed due to time constraints.

## Field Notes

> **[FIELD NOTE — Factor Beniamin Curro, independent grain trader, 2618.311]**
> Diverted through the Porth belt on a supply run and found it a rougher sort of prosperity than the core worlds — hard mining crews, harder bar fights, and a mineral trade in something the local assayers call cherambilth, which fetches a price I found frankly baffling until I saw a sample myself. It has a faint warmth to it that has nothing to do with any radioactivity my instruments could detect, and the miners speak of it with a reverence I have otherwise only heard directed at things people pray to.
>
> I bought a small piece for curiosity's sake. I have caught myself, more than once since, simply holding it while thinking of nothing in particular, and finding the thinking of nothing strangely restful. I do not entirely understand my own behavior around this rock, and I do not entirely trust it either.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Cor. Dassin, Cerberus Corps field agent, 3188.171]**
> Cherambilth's resonance with living minds is well documented in the present era — miners were handling something with the same faint signature long before anyone put a name to it. See [[the-song-beneath-the-stars|the entry on the Song]] for the wider pattern this seems to belong to. Ordinary people kept brushing up against this substrate for generations before it screamed. Nobody with the authority to investigate properly ever did.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Nine-Finger Sable, smuggler (self-described), 3140.304]**
> I carry a piece myself. Don't ask what I paid for it. It's restful, and that's exactly why I don't fully trust it either. Nothing that makes you feel that good is only doing one thing to you.

## Settlement Charter, Okotoks Mining Concern

The undersigned settlers, having secured mineral rights to the Okotoks claim under standard Mandate registry, do hereby establish a self-governing mining collective, to be administered by elected overseer and council, with all extracted value divided per the attached share agreement. The Concern further commits to maintaining full and open records of all extraction activity, available to any registered claim-holder upon request, in perpetuity.

*[hand unknown — Okotoks settlement archive, 2596.220]*

> They do still invoke it. Watched a claim dispute get settled by someone literally quoting this exact charter, word for word, at a council meeting. Made my hair stand up a little, honestly. "In perpetuity" turned out to mean something after all.

## Log, Salvage Captain Working the Belt's Outer Debris Field

Strange wreck out past the third claim marker today — not naval, not civilian, not anything my registry database recognizes at all. Hull geometry all wrong for anyone I know how to build for. Crew wanted to go aboard and strip it for parts. I said no, and I could not entirely explain why beyond a feeling in my gut that some doors are better left shut than opened, whatever's behind them might be worth.

We logged the coordinates and moved on. I have thought about that wreck more than any of the ordinary ones I've actually salvaged.
