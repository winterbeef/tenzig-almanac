## Overview

*Mandate-era summary.* Temperate, breathable, gravity near-standard. Surface given over largely to agriculture — orchard terracing dominates the equatorial band — with the primary settlement of Vivero serving as administrative and educational seat. The University Consortium holds continuous Mandate charter and is the system's sole Class II starport operator. No unusual hazards on record.

## Field Notes

> **[FIELD NOTE — Factor Beniamin Curro, independent grain trader, 2589.301]**
> Lycos is a soft landing after Perope's escort fees, and I say that with real feeling, having just paid them. Breathable air, gentle gravity, more orchard than city in most districts — the sort of world where a man can walk from the shuttle pad to a meal without armor, which after the season I've had counts as luxury.
>
> The place runs on two things: fruit and vanity. The fruit I understand — the lowland terraces around Vivero produce a stone-fruit brandy I've taken pains to secure exclusive carriage rights for. The vanity is the University Consortium, which every faculty on six worlds insists is the finest research institution in the cluster, and which every faculty on six worlds is wrong about, except possibly this once.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Surveyor-Adept Yuen Petrakis, Terran Mandate Cartographic Office, 2604.118]**
> Assessment: Lycos is stable, prosperous, and uneventful. The University Consortium holds an old Mandate charter and guards it jealously; three centuries of accumulated grant money have built something between a library and a fortress in the hills above Vivero, and the faculty senate treats their archives with a possessiveness I have rarely seen outside a dragon hoard.
>
> There is a persistent rumor among the porters that the Consortium keeps something in its deep vaults that predates the university itself by a wide margin — pre-Mandate work, possibly, or something older still. I asked three separate faculty members about this. Two laughed. One did not answer at all, which interested me rather more than either laugh.

> **[FIELD NOTE — R. Achterberg, independent hauler, 3142.077]**
> Nobody on Lycos today can point me to anything called a "University Consortium." Vivero's still the biggest city on the planet, still sits on top of some kind of old vault system in the hills, but whatever runs it now isn't wearing the old name. Make of that what you will — I mostly wanted a bed and a hot meal and got both, so I'm not complaining.

## Proclamation, Wildlands Compact (Cardoza Village and Environs)

Let it be known to all traders, tax-farmers, and University men entering the Wildlands beyond the third terrace: the Compact does not recognize Consortium law, does not pay Consortium tithes, and will not be surveyed, catalogued, or "preserved" by any faculty expedition without an invitation freely given.

We have watched what your scholars call "preservation" done to three villages upriver. We have counted what was left afterward, which was very little, and none of it villages.

## Recovered Correspondence, Nightmarket Fixer, Addressee Unknown

You want to know if there's money in orchard futures on Lycos. There's money in everything on Lycos if you know which door to knock on and which questions not to ask twice. The Consortium men buy information the same as anyone, they just pretend it's "research funding." Ask around the lower Nightmarket for a woman who trades in old paper — real paper, pre-digitized, the kind nobody's bothered scanning. She'll tell you the Consortium pays triple for anything that mentions the word "Archive," and won't say why.
