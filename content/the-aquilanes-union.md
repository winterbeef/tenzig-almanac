Not every post-Scream power grew out of desperation. The Aquilanes Union is the counter-example most often cited: a mercantile alliance that built something resembling a navy not to conquer, but to keep the lanes open for everyone paying dues.

The Union formed in the generations after the Scream out of a simple problem: independent trade routes need protecting, and no single world could afford it alone. Membership dues fund a standing patrol force, and in return members get escort priority and a say in route governance. It has grown steadily since, and its shipbuilding contracts — a substantial share of them placed with the [[the-shipyards-of-eurymem|Shipyards of Eurymem]] — represent some of the largest standing orders in the sector.
