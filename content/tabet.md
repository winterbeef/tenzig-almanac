## Overview

*Mandate-era summary.* The dominant population center of the Perope cluster and its cultural anchor. Continuous settlement for centuries prior to survey; local governance organized around a Cultural Board with limited administrative authority beyond tourism and civic festival licensing. No unusual hazards on record. Recommend as primary crew liberty stop for vessels transiting the cluster.

## Field Notes

> **[FIELD NOTE — Tabet Cultural Board tourist pamphlet, 3rd printing, 2607.190]**
> Welcome, traveler, to Tabet — a world proud to call itself the beating heart of the [[perope|Perope]] cluster! Whatever business brings you through our docks, we invite you to stay an extra night for the Lantern Processional, held every third evening in the Old Quarter, where six hundred years of continuous settlement have produced a culture unmatched anywhere in this stretch of the map. (The Cultural Board would like to note that [[eurymem-and-hypsila|Hypsila]]'s tourism office makes an identical claim about their own Processional, and that this claim is, as with most things Hypsila says, simply untrue.)

> **[FIELD NOTE — J. Praast, Mandate Cartographic Office (ret.), 3134.052]**
> "Cultural Board" is doing a lot of quiet work in that old pamphlet. Any world can throw a lantern parade. It takes a government office to decide a culture needs a board to certify it, usually right around the time somebody figures out how to sell tickets to it. Whatever's left of that board today, if anything, I couldn't find it in three weeks of asking.

> **[FIELD NOTE — R. Achterberg, independent hauler, 3142.078]**
> Say what you want about the certifying — Tabet's been the liveliest port in this cluster longer than any board's been around to take credit for it, and it still is. Some things don't need a stamp.
