# The Heirs of Carderas

Every haunted stretch of the sector has a name attached to the trouble, and out past the quieter lanes, that name is Carderas. Nobody agrees on who Carderas actually was. Everyone agrees on what the Heirs do.

The Heirs are less a single organization than a loose banner flown by several crews who've adopted the name and, by most accounts, some shared rules of conduct around it — targets, territory, and what happens to prisoners. Two names recur often enough in shipping-lane chatter to count as this entry's actual subjects.

**Violeta "Vee" Obregon** is spoken of with something close to respect even by people she's robbed — disciplined, allegedly opposed to unnecessary killing, and, per more than one secondhand account, particular about which cargo she'll touch.

**Javier Roma** carries a very different reputation: aggressive, unpredictable, and blamed — accurately or not — for most of the incidents that make captains reroute rather than risk the lane at all.
