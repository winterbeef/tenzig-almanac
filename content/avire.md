## Field Notes

> **[FIELD NOTE — commissioning officer, TMN Anemoi's Gift, 2598.003]**
> *Anemoi's Gift* enters active naval service this cycle, assigned to the [[perope|Perope]] corridor as a forward resupply and repair platform for escort and survey vessels operating the [[tabet|Tabet]] approach. She carries a crew of one hundred forty, full fabrication and repair bays, and enough reaction mass to keep three escort squadrons fueled through an extended deployment.
>
> Her captain, on commissioning, is recorded only as remarking that she is "an ugly ship for an ugly job, and I mean that as the highest compliment I know how to give a vessel."

> **[FIELD NOTE — junior officer, TMN Anemoi's Gift, name redacted by a later hand, 2599.140]**
> Third week on the Perope corridor and I am beginning to understand why the old hands call this posting "the ugly job" without a trace of irony. We refuel, rearm, and repair anything that limps in from the corridor, ask no questions we're not ordered to ask, and watch the survey traffic go by in both directions like a slow parade of people who all seem to know something we don't.
>
> Overheard two escort pilots talking dockside about "the second rock work" in low voices, the way you talk about something you're being paid well not to talk about at all. Didn't ask. Wrote it down instead, because some habits from the Academy never leave you.

> **[FIELD NOTE — Nine-Finger Sable, smuggler (self-described), 3140.301]**
> Drank in that bar for a solid decade of my life before somebody told me it used to be a warship's actual wardroom. Explains a lot about the acoustics, honestly. Explains nothing about the drinks, which have always been terrible.

> **[FIELD NOTE — M. Okonkwo-Reyes, Archive Adept, 2nd Rank, 3176.088]**
> For the record, since nobody else here bothered: the description of Avire station that circulates today — a converted Ticonderoga-class resupply hull, holding at that Lagrange point for going on thirty years under its current keeper — matches the old commissioning record closely enough that I don't believe it's coincidence. If I'm right, the bar they call the Wardroom is sitting more or less exactly where the actual wardroom used to be. Make of that what you will. I find it strangely moving and I am not a sentimental woman.
