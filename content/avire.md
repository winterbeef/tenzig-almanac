## From the Commissioning Record of the TMN *Ticonderoga*-Class Resupply Vessel *Anemoi's Gift*

*Anemoi's Gift* enters active naval service this cycle, assigned to the [[perope|Perope]] corridor as a forward resupply and repair platform for escort and survey vessels operating the [[tabet|Tabet]] approach. She carries a crew of one hundred forty, full fabrication and repair bays, and enough reaction mass to keep three escort squadrons fueled through an extended deployment.

Her captain, on commissioning, is recorded only as remarking that she is "an ugly ship for an ugly job, and I mean that as the highest compliment I know how to give a vessel."

> **Corvid:** I want to be careful here, because I could easily be wrong, and I would rather be cautious than dramatic. But the description of Avire station that circulates today — a converted Ticonderoga-class resupply hull, holding at that Lagrange point for going on thirty years under its current keeper — matches this commissioning record closely enough that I do not think it is coincidence. If I am right, then the bar in Avire's common hold, the one they call the Wardroom, is sitting more or less exactly where a warship's actual wardroom used to be.
>
> **Two-Coin:** I've drunk in that bar. I did not know that when I did. I am choosing not to feel any particular way about it now, either.
>
> **Cinder:** "An ugly ship for an ugly job" is a captain's line, and I notice it's the captain's line that survived, not the names of the hundred forty crew who actually kept that corridor fueled. The mission gets remembered fondly. The people who ran it, then or now, mostly don't get a line in the record at all.

## From the Duty Log of a Junior Officer Aboard *Anemoi's Gift*, Name Redacted by a Later Hand

Third week on the Perope corridor and I am beginning to understand why the old hands call this posting "the ugly job" without a trace of irony. We refuel, rearm, and repair anything that limps in from the corridor, ask no questions we're not ordered to ask, and watch the survey traffic go by in both directions like a slow parade of people who all seem to know something we don't.

Overheard two escort pilots talking dockside about "the second rock work" in low voices, the way you talk about something you're being paid well not to talk about at all. Didn't ask. Wrote it down instead, because some habits from the Academy never leave you.

> **Corvid:** Whatever this officer overheard, they had the good sense not to write down more than they did. I respect it, and I will follow their example rather better than Master Oduya's, [[perope|quoted elsewhere in this volume]].
>
> **Two-Coin:** Corvid means "Oduya kept poking at it and I'm not going to." Which, fine, fair, but somebody should. I've thought about it more than once.

## Scratched Into a Support Strut, Avire Station, Present Era — Included Here Against Better Judgment

WE WERE HERE FIRST. YOU WILL NOT BE THE LAST.

*[hand unknown, and — unlike everything else quoted in this volume — plainly not a pre-Scream fragment at all]*

> **Corvid:** I include this one out of order, against my own rule, because I found it in the same mural wall where the ship's old commissioning plate is bolted, and because I have no idea who wrote it, or when, or in what language it was first carved before whatever hand rendered it into the trade-tongue we read today. It unsettles me. I leave it here unexplained because I have nothing honest to explain it with.
>
> **Two-Coin:** I've seen that strut. I didn't touch it, and I'm not proud of how fast I walked past it either.
>
> **Cinder:** I'd touch it. Every authority in this book has told somebody not to touch something without ever once explaining why, and I've stopped taking "don't" as a complete argument.
>
> **Two-Coin:** There's a difference between defiance and a bad idea. This might be both.
