# The Council of the Shadowmen

Five names that matter more than most to anyone booking passage through [[perope|Perope]]. The Guild of Pilots — called "the Shadowmen" by half the cluster and "the Escort and Salvage Cooperative" by the half that files paperwork — is governed by a council of five, and this dossier is that council.

**Marek Solva**, 63 — the eldest active council member, forty years flying escort through the Perope crossing, a scarred left forearm he doesn't discuss. Speaks last in council, and decisively. Publicly the most neutral of the five on any contentious question.

**Dessa Pryn**, 41 — second-generation Guild, the council's sharpest navigator, and its most vocal skeptic of anything that threatens the Guild's leverage over the crossing. Runs numbers on every proposal before anyone else has finished reading it.

**Foret**, 48 — arrived twelve years ago from off-cluster, the Guild's best active pilot, and by wide agreement uninterested in politics of any kind. Wants to see the hardware, not hear the argument.

**Vita Solva**, 31 — Marek's daughter, earned her seat rather than inherited it, and is generally read as the council's swing vote. Privately sympathetic to reform of some of the Guild's older, more closed-door traditions.

**Bollen Pryn**, 67 — semi-retired, Dessa's uncle, attends council erratically and speaks bluntly when he does. Guild wisdom holds he's right more often than the odds should allow.

> I fly for these five, so take that as you like, but I'll say it straight: you want a fair hearing from the council, bring Marek something he can weigh calmly.
